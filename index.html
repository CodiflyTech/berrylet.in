Our Brand Story

The Berrylet Heritage

Tradition & Craftsmanship

Founded under the corporate governance of Aruma Products Private Limited, Berrylet was born out of a deep appreciation for traditional bakery craftsmanship and quality confectionery. Rooted in the rich cultural landscape of Assam, India, our brand is dedicated to bringing thoughtfully crafted, artisanal cookies and snacks to households across the nation.

We believe that exceptional baked goods begin with meticulous attention to detail, carefully balanced recipes, and an unwavering commitment to culinary excellence.

Crafted with Care and Precision

Every single batch of Berrylet cookies—whether our signature Coconut, Chocolate Chip, Roasted Jeera, or Cornflakes variants—is crafted using time-tested baking techniques. We partner with specialized manufacturing facilities, such as Nine Star Enterprises, to ensure rigorous quality control at every stage of production.

From selecting fine ingredients like premium maida, desiccated coconut, and pure butter to executing precise baking temperatures, our process is designed to deliver a consistently delightful crunch and rich flavor profile in every bite.

Our Quality Promise

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified enterprise, Aruma Products Private Limited operates under strict quality management frameworks. We adhere strictly to FSSAI standards, ensuring complete regulatory compliance, transparency, and safety across all our product lines.

Berrylet stands for reliability, consistency, and a passion for fine food, making every tea-time moment special for our customers.
